# Yuna Console app icon

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-10-31. `startup-jacket-v2.png` was visually inspected and used as a character/style reference. The original master is `app-icon-v1.png`, with transparency preserved.

`npm run build:icons` uses Electron nativeImage to resize the master into `../kuma-console.png` (256 px) and `../kuma-console.ico` (16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px PNG frames). These are used by the browser favicon, sidebar brand, desktop title bar, splash, Electron window, system tray and packaged EXE. The old SVG is retained as an unused legacy asset.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production square app icon for Kuma Console, 1024 by 1024. Reference image is a character identity reference ONLY, not an edit target. Draw a new compact charming portrait of the SAME original adult anime character Yuna: silver-white shoulder-length bob, one curved ahoge, ice blue eyes, small white bear hair clip at her left temple, black choker with tiny bear charm, hints of white opaque top and cyan techwear collar. Calm friendly slight smile, looking toward viewer. Icon-specific composition: frontal centered head and upper shoulders fills 85 percent of the square, large clearly readable face and eyes, hair silhouette entirely inside frame with 6 percent margin, bear clip clearly visible. Simplified polished Japanese anime rendering, clean bold outer contours and restrained large shading shapes for readability at 16 to 48 pixels, avoid busy fine details. Background: a single dark desaturated navy rounded-square app tile with softly rounded corners, subtle icy cyan rim light; genuinely transparent outside rounded tile. Strong separation of pale hair against navy. Symmetric balanced visual weight, professional desktop application icon. No text, no letter K, no initials, no words, no logo overlays, no watermark, no scene or holograms, no extra characters, no photorealism. Preserve Yuna's recognizable face and palette; not generic mascot animal.
